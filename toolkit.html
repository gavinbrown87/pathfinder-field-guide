The Pathfinder Field Guide

Know the ground before you cross it.

Your goal is noble. The terrain doesn't care.

Raising the money, serving more people, growing the mission — that's the easy part to want. The hard part is the ground between here and there: the board that won't function, the books that won't reconcile, the team stretched too thin. These free tools help you see that terrain honestly — so a good goal doesn't die in it.

Most nonprofits are small. More than nine in ten run on under a million dollars a year, with no development director, no consultant on retainer, and a board doing the work part-time. The skills still matter — they're just hard to come by. These tools teach the thinking and do the heavy lifting with you, so the size of your budget stops deciding the quality of your fundraising.
